Why I built this

An about page, and the reasoning behind the whole thing.

The reason this exists

I made this site because the work I do kept disappearing. A project would get finished on a Tuesday, get graded, and then sit in a folder where nobody — including me — would ever look at it again.

Omorose Inc. is the fix for that. It is one place where the things I build actually live: the assignments, the side projects, the small experiments that did not go anywhere but taught me something on the way. Having a place to put work changes how you do the work. When you know something will be seen, you finish it properly.

What motivated me

I got tired of being a person who was going to learn this. I wanted to be a person who had built something.

Honestly, the first push was frustration. Web development looked like a wall of things I did not understand, and every tutorial assumed I already knew the last five. It was easier to feel behind than to start.

So I started with something small enough that I could not talk myself out of it: one page, one stylesheet. This page. It turns out that most of the wall is not that tall once you are standing next to it. HTML is a way of naming your content, CSS is a way of describing how it should look, and everything after that is a variation on those two ideas.

The plan from here is straightforward. Keep building, keep putting it somewhere public, and let the site grow as the skills do. If a future version of this page looks nothing like this one, that is the point.
