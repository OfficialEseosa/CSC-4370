Why I built this

I built Omorose Inc. because my work kept disappearing. Projects got finished, got graded, and then sat in a folder nobody opened again.

This site is where that work lives instead. Having somewhere to put things changes how you make them. When you know something will be seen, you finish it properly.

The motivation was mostly frustration. Web development looked like a wall of things I did not understand, and it was easier to feel behind than to start. So I started small enough that I could not talk myself out of it: one page, one stylesheet. This page. Most of the wall is not that tall once you are standing next to it.
